(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2z-7pIj57f8, full transcript read) and the "8 Jev Use Cases" snippet; Cole Medin and AI Jason Jev videos have no transcript. **Why it didn't clear the bar.**

One critique notes the reference answers are the average of GPT-6 Astra and Fable 5.1, so it measures agreement with those models, not correctness (https://anthonymaio.substack.com/p/jev-the-language-model-that-wont).
