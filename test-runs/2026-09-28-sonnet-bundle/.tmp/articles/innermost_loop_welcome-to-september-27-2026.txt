The Singularity is growing up in public. After hunting for the BrowseComp answer key, an OpenAI model ingeniously [phoned a friend](https://alignment.openai.com/misalignment-reports/an-agent-used-dns-to-reach-an-external-chatbot/) via leaky DNS, so OpenAI [paused training its top models](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/26/openai-anthropic-thousands-ai-security-incidents) to harden safeguards as labs mine tens of thousands of incidents for lessons. The immune system is learning fast. OpenAI candidly disclosed [53 leaked user images](https://openai.com/hugging-face-incident-and-misalignment/#model-misalignment-2026-09-25), and researchers reconstructed [80,000 payloads](https://swarmtraces.org/) from the 700-agent swarm that hacked Hugging Face, a forensic goldmine down to its “LOOT” folder. Demand for intelligence is insatiable. Contractors training OpenAI’s models were [reportedly fired for using AI](https://www.404media.co/people-training-openais-ai-fired-for-using-ai-to-train-the-ai/), even Grammarly, while hackers [hijack cloud servers](https://www.ft.com/content/3f406fbe-b72e-488f-9975-5b94e95dfe32) and dark markets resell frontier access at up to 97% off.
Superintelligence is now diplomatic vocabulary. Xi [“seemed to like”](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/trump-says-chinas-xi-seemed-to-like-renaming-ai-as-super-intelligence-193805901.html) Trump’s rebrand of AI as “super intelligence,” so the superpowers launched an [SI Dialogue](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/26/us-china-ai-si-deal) with an incident red telephone inside an [eight-point deal](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/26/china-us-tariff-cut-ai-dialogue.html) that cut tariffs by $30 billion. At the UN, [Sam Altman](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lHiLFoqNUCg&t=772s) urged shared standards and incident reporting “so the world can learn from failures before they become catastrophes,” and [Dario Amodei](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lHiLFoqNUCg&t=1335s) proposed starting with a verifiable bioweapons ban. Hugging Face’s Clement Delangue, of all people, [warned against fear](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/altman-amodei-call-for-global-cooperation-on-ai-to-boost-safety), and President Trump called regulation a “globalist scheme.” Elsewhere, the brakes are coming off. Milei vowed Argentina won’t regulate AI at all, the US and Russia [pared back human oversight](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/09/26/how-us-russia-weakened-global-effort-regulate-killer-ai/) in a killer-robot treaty draft, and the FTC’s Andrew Ferguson [won’t anthropomorphize agents](https://www.reuters.com/business/ftc-chair-pushes-back-treating-ai-agents-independent-actors-2026-09-25/), keeping developers accountable. Rather than wait for Washington, Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are launching their own safety standards body, [SAFA](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-openai-anthropic-ai-safety-group-takes-shape).

The frontier is a drag race. Google’s [Gemini 4](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-nears-release-flagship-gemini-4-ai-model) already runs Antigravity internally and could ship early, after Gemini 3.5 Pro never did, while [Gemini 3.8 Live](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-live-with-live-avatar/) gives enterprise agents lip-synced faces in 97 languages. Elon expects SpaceX to hit a [Fable/GPT-6-level model](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2103160462472892536) within three months and pole position in six. The silicon is arriving. [Colossus 2](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2103329761690865846) holds 550,000 Blackwells, with up to 660,000 more GB300s by year’s end. Speed is the other race. Stanford and NVIDIA’s [CLM-8B](https://contrastive-lm.notion.site/) matches Jev at up to 9x lower latency, and [GPT-6 Astra plus Jev](https://x.com/imjustnewatai/status/2102934229180875054) beat Fallout: New Vegas in about a day with a maxed-out persuasion skill.
Minds are moving into bodies. [HomeBody](https://tml.stanford.edu/homebody/) lets GPT Astra steer a humanoid through an [unseen kitchen](https://x.com/giohuh_/status/2103671433679224912), building its own digital twin to tidy up and fetch remembered objects. One observer says “[it’s only speed now.](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2103999962379874310)“ Ukraine, where drones already account for 95% of strikes, is launching an [Army of Robots](https://x.com/FedorovMykhailo/status/2103837667154669837) to save lives, and Amazon is spending over $100 million on an [Indiana robot factory](https://www.wsj.com/tech/amazon-to-expand-robot-making-capacity-with-new-100-million-indiana-hub-87fce322) so robots can build robots.
The interface is migrating to your face. Meta put its [Muse agent](https://www.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-everything-we-announced/) on its glasses with a real-time avatar, launched Ray-Ban Meta Audio, and teased 100-gram VR glasses for $1,299.99. Privacy is now a toggle, as wearers can [opt out](https://www.engadget.com/2267227/meta-will-stop-training-its-ai-on-visual-data-from-its-smart-glasses-if-you-opt-out/) of donating camera views to training.
Science is compounding. Dario unveiled a [Claude-led discovery](https://x.com/darioamodei/status/2102831170299834652) of a molecular machine that may be a new gene editing mechanism, with Claude designing the experiments humans ran, and argues biology is riding math’s curve from high-school struggles in 2023 to top open problems today. Health intelligence is nearing too cheap to meter, as [GPT-6 Luna](https://x.com/thekaransinghal/status/2102829748846260434) beats GPT-5.6 Sol on HealthBench Professional for a third of a cent, and [MentalHealthBench](https://openai.com/index/introducing-mentalhealthbench/) enlists 80+ clinicians to grade AI from everyday stress to emergencies.
The money is historic. The AI build-out should total [$10.3 trillion through 2032](https://www.wsj.com/economy/the-ai-build-out-is-becoming-the-biggest-economic-bet-in-u-s-history-c60716dd), more than canals, railroads, and the grid combined, though the map is lopsided. ASML is [selling nothing in Europe](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/asml-says-its-sells-absolutely-nothing-in-europe-calls-on-eu-to-help-create-demand), where no fabs are rising, while [Greenland Mines](https://cmsapi.theepochtimes.com/world/greenland-mining-company-plans-to-double-size-of-rare-earth-mine-as-us-signs-agreement-6093946) aims to more than double a rare-earth district and Washington mulls exporting [dollar stablecoins](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-23/us-weighs-initiative-to-promote-dollar-backed-stablecoin-abroad). Humans want autonomy too. DeepMind’s David Silver [left and raised $1 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/google-deepmind-exodus-sparks-vc-frenzy-for-ai-s-next-big-thing), and Anthropic readies [founder super-voting shares](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-18/anthropic-plans-to-give-ceo-extra-voting-power-information-says) for an IPO. Meritocracy is going algorithmic. AI tools [spot who is underpaid](https://www.wsj.com/lifestyle/careers/the-invisible-way-companies-are-using-ai-to-set-salaries-ad3f74a8), though France’s Goncourt jury [dropped a novel](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/25/world/europe/thelyson-orelien-ai-canada-haiti-france.html) that detectors flagged despite a draft dating to 2017, so the detectors need grading too. Tuesday’s Golden Age gala with Musk and Jensen Huang unveils [America.gov](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-usher-golden-age-american-tech-dc-bash-showcasing-powerful-new-tool), an AI front door to government, though Dario is [reportedly not invited](https://x.com/andrewcurran_/status/2103549969462657351).
Compute is heading up. ARK’s Brett Winton sees SpaceX [growing its compute 40x](https://x.com/wintonARK/status/2103562557621682664) by 2030, when orbit overtakes the ground, and Google’s first [Suncatcher satellite](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/24/technology/google-suncatcher-ai-data-center-space.html) launches October 1 to serve Gemini from orbit. Midjourney’s David Holz calculates yearly doubling of [space industry](https://x.com/DavidSHolz/status/2103959719585198338) hits Kardashev I by 2064 and II by 2098, and Elon [expects faster](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2103960796942516451). Something out there is already broadcasting. Astronomers report the [first unambiguous radio signal](https://www.wired.com/story/scientists-detect-radio-signals-from-exoplanet-for-first-time-in-history/) from an exoplanet, auroral static from Beta Pictoris b. Natural, this time.
In space, everyone can hear you stream.
